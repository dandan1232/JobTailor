## Design Context

### Users
JobTailor serves software engineers with roughly one to five years of experience who are preparing targeted job applications. They need to turn a truthful career history into evidence-backed resume content for a specific job description without inventing experience.

### Brand Personality
Concise, technical, and trustworthy. The product should feel calm and precise, helping users make controlled decisions rather than presenting AI as magic.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light, work-focused interface with cool tinted neutrals and a restrained blue-green accent. Favor clear information hierarchy, compact controls, and visible evidence links. Avoid decorative gradients, oversized marketing typography, glass effects, dark neon AI styling, and excessive cards.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with the user's work, not product promotion.
2. Make every generated claim traceable to source experience.
3. Use color and motion only to communicate status or hierarchy.
4. Keep the primary workflow usable on both desktop and mobile.
5. Preserve user control at consequential steps; the system advises but does not apply automatically.
